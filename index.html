Hamare Baare Mein

Asli Punjabi swad, seedha dil se

Sardar Ji Dhaba mein aapka tah dil se swagat hai! Hamare yahan har dish traditional Punjabi recipes se banayi jaati hai — bilkul ghar jaisi. 2008 se hum apne pyaar se khana pakate hain aur har customer ko family ki tarah maante hain.

Hamare fresh tandoor mein bani rotiyaan, desi ghee ki dal makhani, thandi mast lassi — yeh sab milke ek aisa anubhav deta hai jo aap kabhi nahi bhoolenge. Hazratganj, Lucknow ke dil mein, hum aapka intezaar karte hain!
